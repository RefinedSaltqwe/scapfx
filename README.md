## ✨ About

Scap Creative is a photography-focused eCommerce platform built for creators who want to achieve refined, film-inspired aesthetics with minimal effort.

The platform offers premium Lightroom presets, photography resources, and digital products designed to streamline editing workflows and help photographers develop a consistent visual style.

## 📸 Philosophy

Scap Creative was created to help photographers achieve soft, elegant, and timeless imagery without spending hours editing.

The products are designed for photographers who love:

- Film-inspired colors
- Soft contrast
- Natural skin tones
- Balanced exposure
- Atmospheric storytelling
- Consistent editing workflows

Photography is more than creating beautiful images — it's about capturing meaningful moments and telling compelling stories.
